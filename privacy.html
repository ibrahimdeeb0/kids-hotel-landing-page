Website privacy.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This page explains privacy on the Kids Hotel informational website. Kids Hotel operates under A.H.M HINDI LTD., a registered limited company based in Israel.

Scope

This notice applies to this website only. It does not describe the data practices of a mobile application or any separate nursery service.

Browsing this website

This website contains company and contact information. It has no registration, contact forms, advertising, or analytics scripts. The website code does not set cookies or use browser storage, and does not request children’s information.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website. The provider’s own privacy terms also apply to that processing.

Contacting us

The email and telephone links open your chosen email or calling service. If you contact us, you choose what information to share. Please avoid sending children’s personal information or sensitive documents in an initial enquiry.
